17 hour outage Sep 14, 2021

On September 14, 2021 a surge of bot transactions during a token sale on the Raydium platform overwhelmed validators. The network stopped producing blocks and was down for about 17 hours until validators coordinated a restart.

2021 peak Nov 6, 2021

Solana rose from under 2 dollars in early 2021 to its highest prices that November, on the back of NFT and DeFi growth and heavy backing from FTX and Alameda Research. The date is the highest daily close in our series for 2021.
