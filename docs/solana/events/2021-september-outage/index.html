Outage · Sep 14, 2021

17 hour outage

A flood of bot transactions halts the network for about 17 hours.

SOL close on Sep 14, 2021
$158.64
Source: Coinbase Exchange SOL/USD daily close, 2021-09-14

On September 14, 2021 a surge of bot transactions during a token sale on the Raydium platform overwhelmed validators. The network stopped producing blocks and was down for about 17 hours until validators coordinated a restart.

It was the first of several major outages. See the outage history page for the full list.
