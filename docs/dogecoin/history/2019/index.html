Musk: Dogecoin might be my fav Apr 2, 2019

On April 2, 2019 Elon Musk posted that Dogecoin might be his favorite cryptocurrency, calling it "pretty cool". It was the start of years of posts that would move the price. Our price series starts in July 2019, so this date has no sourced price.
